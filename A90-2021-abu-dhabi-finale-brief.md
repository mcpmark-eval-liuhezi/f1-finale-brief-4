## 4. Championship Context for the Retro

Max Verstappen (Red Bull) and Lewis Hamilton (Mercedes) arrived at Yas Marina level on **162 points**. Verstappen started from pole (grid 1) and won ahead of Hamilton, who finished second — 2.256s behind within the lap's first corners — and ahead of Carlos Sainz in third. Hamilton's fastest lap was rank 3 (lap 43, 1:26.615); Verstappen took the fastest lap of the race (lap 39, 1:26.103). Verstappen's win secured the 2021 World Drivers' Championship.
